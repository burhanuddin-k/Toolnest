Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

ToolNest image editing, word counting, age calculation and password generation operate locally in your browser. Those inputs are not intentionally uploaded to our servers.

QR Code Generation

QR codes use the third-party QRServer API (api.qrserver.com). Text entered into that tool is sent to this external service. Do not enter sensitive information.

Hosting and future advertising

The hosting provider may collect standard server logs. This version does not contain advertising or analytics scripts. This policy must be updated before introducing ads, analytics, cookies or other data collection.
